# The AI Slop Taxonomy: 20 Antipatterns & Diagnostic Signals

This document catalogs recurring interface design flaws generated by AI models and copy-paste boilerplate, providing exact code diagnostics and remediation tactics.

---

## 1. The Purple/Indigo Nebula (`SMELL-01`)
- **Symptom**: Large radial gradient meshes or absolute-positioned blurred divs (`bg-purple-600/20 blur-3xl rounded-full`) behind text.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `from-purple-500 to-indigo-500`, `blur-2xl`, `blur-3xl`, `radial-gradient` in root layout.
- **Remedy**: Strip background blurs. Use solid or subtly textured neutral canvases (`zinc-950` or `#fafafa`) with intentional focal lighting if needed.

## 2. Card-in-Card Nesting (`SMELL-02`)
- **Symptom**: Wrapping every sub-section, label, and statistic into a discrete bordered box.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Nested elements with `border`, `rounded-*`, and `bg-*` stacked 2+ levels deep.
- **Remedy**: Remove inner card wrappers. Use tabular lists, hairline dividers (`border-b`), or Gestalt spacing.

## 3. Border-Radius Overdrive (`SMELL-03`)
- **Symptom**: Applying `rounded-2xl` (16px) or `rounded-3xl` (24px) to small elements (buttons, table rows, tags).
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `rounded-2xl`, `rounded-3xl`, `border-radius: 20px+` on elements with height `< 48px`.
- **Remedy**: Scale radius proportionally to component height. Small controls get 4px–6px (`rounded-md`); containers get 8px (`rounded-lg`).

## 4. Frosted Glass Soup (`SMELL-04`)
- **Symptom**: Excessive `backdrop-blur-*` on cards, sidebars, and panels, causing washed-out contrast and GPU lag.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Multiple instances of `backdrop-blur-md` or `backdrop-filter: blur(...)` across main page content.
- **Remedy**: Restrict backdrop blur strictly to fixed floating navigation headers. Use opaque, elevated solid surface tokens for cards.

## 5. Typographic Flaccidity (`SMELL-05`)
- **Symptom**: Headings and body copy look almost identical in weight and size; zero optical rhythm.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: H1 is only `text-xl` or `font-semibold` while body text is `text-base` without leading or tracking adjustments.
- **Remedy**: Implement a strict mathematical modular scale (e.g., Major Second). H1 gets `text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight leading-tight`; body gets `text-base leading-relaxed max-w-prose`.

## 6. The Predictable SaaS Hero (`SMELL-06`)
- **Symptom**: Centered pill badge (`✨ AI 2.0`), 3-line generic title, two equal-width buttons, and a tilted dashboard screenshot with purple shadow.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `text-center mx-auto max-w-3xl` hero wrapper with two adjacent primary-styled buttons and emoji badges.
- **Remedy**: Use an asymmetric layout (e.g. 60/40 split), lead with an interactive preview or tangible product artifact, and provide one clear primary CTA.

## 7. Hollow Buzzword Copy (`SMELL-07`)
- **Symptom**: Placeholder text dense with *supercharge, seamless, next-gen, revolutionary, unleash, cutting-edge*.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Phrases like "Supercharge your workflow seamlessly" or "Next-gen intelligent solution".
- **Remedy**: Replace with concrete domain metrics, direct capabilities, and active verbs ("Process 10k events/sec", "Continuous deployment for monorepos").

## 8. Button Weight Identity Crisis (`SMELL-08`)
- **Symptom**: Primary, secondary, filter, and cancel buttons all have the same solid or gradient background.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Multiple buttons in the same container having identical `bg-primary` or `bg-gradient-*` classes.
- **Remedy**: Strictly limit to one primary button per view context. Style secondary buttons with subtle borders/surfaces and tertiary buttons as ghost links.

## 9. Muddy Dark Mode (`SMELL-09`)
- **Symptom**: Pure pitch-black `#000` canvas paired with dark gray `#4b5563` text that fails WCAG AA contrast.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Contrast ratio < 4.5:1 between text and background in dark mode.
- **Remedy**: Use layered dark neutrals (`zinc-950` canvas, `zinc-900` surface) with high-contrast text (`zinc-100` primary, `zinc-400` secondary).

## 10. Gradient Text Clip Abuse (`SMELL-10`)
- **Symptom**: Headlines rendered in multi-color gradient clips (`bg-clip-text text-transparent bg-gradient-to-r`).
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `bg-clip-text text-transparent` used across multiple headings or body text.
- **Remedy**: Use solid, high-contrast typography (`text-foreground`). If an accent is needed, highlight a single keyword with brand color or typographic weight.

## 11. Unanchored Drop Shadows (`SMELL-11`)
- **Symptom**: Large, diffuse black shadows with no directional consistency (`shadow-2xl` on every card).
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Heavy drop shadows placed on flat dark-mode surfaces where light sources make no optical sense.
- **Remedy**: Replace diffuse shadows with crisp 1px borders (`border border-border/80`) or subtle, tight elevation shadows.

## 12. Pill Badge Infestation (`SMELL-12`)
- **Symptom**: 3 to 5 rounded pill tags attached to every headline or table row.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Clustered `rounded-full px-3 py-1 text-xs` badges in multiple bright colors.
- **Remedy**: Consolidate status into a single subtle indicator (e.g. 6px status dot + concise label).

## 13. Gratuitous Animated Entry Delays (`SMELL-13`)
- **Symptom**: Staggered cards taking 1–2 seconds to fade into view on initial page load.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `animation-delay` or Framer Motion stagger children with delays > 100ms.
- **Remedy**: Render content immediately. Reserve transitions strictly for user-triggered state changes (<150ms).

## 14. Perpetual Motion Distractions (`SMELL-14`)
- **Symptom**: Buttons bouncing, pulsating, or spinning while idle in normal state.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `animate-bounce` or `animate-pulse` applied to resting interactive buttons or cards.
- **Remedy**: Remove idle animations. Provide crisp, tactile `:hover` and `:active` scale feedback instead.

## 15. The 1400px Wide Paragraph (`SMELL-15`)
- **Symptom**: Body text stretching from edge to edge on large screens, tiring the reader's eyes.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `<p>` tags without `max-w-*` inside fluid full-width containers.
- **Remedy**: Add `max-w-prose` (65ch) or `max-w-2xl` to readable copy containers.

## 16. Missing Keyboard Focus Affordance (`SMELL-16`)
- **Symptom**: Removing focus rings via `outline-none` without providing `focus-visible:ring-2`.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `outline-none` or `focus:outline-none` without adjacent `focus-visible:ring-*`.
- **Remedy**: Add `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary focus-visible:ring-offset-2`.

## 17. Broken Mobile Table Stacks (`SMELL-17`)
- **Symptom**: Multi-column data tables causing horizontal page scroll on mobile screens.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: `<table>` without a wrapper having `overflow-x-auto`.
- **Remedy**: Wrap tables in responsive overflow containers or transform data rows into mobile KPI cards.

## 18. Micro-Spacing Chaos (`SMELL-18`)
- **Symptom**: Arbitrary, inconsistent padding and margin values (e.g. mixing 7px, 11px, 23px).
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Unsystematic spacing classes (`p-[13px]`, `mt-[7px]`).
- **Remedy**: Standardize on a 4px/8px rhythm (`p-2`, `p-3`, `p-4`, `p-6`).

## 19. Symmetrical Boredom (`SMELL-19`)
- **Symptom**: Monotonous, repetitive grids where every section has the exact same layout structure.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Identical 3-column or 4-column card blocks repeated 4+ times down a single page.
- **Remedy**: Vary content pacing: combine high-density lists, asymmetric feature callouts, and clean data tables.

## 20. Missing Interactive States (`SMELL-20`)
- **Symptom**: Buttons that do not react when hovered, pressed, or disabled.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Interactive elements lacking `:hover`, `:active`, or `:disabled` rules.
- **Remedy**: Provide subtle background shifts (`hover:bg-accent`), tactile click states (`active:scale-[0.98]`), and dimmed disabled states (`disabled:opacity-50`).
